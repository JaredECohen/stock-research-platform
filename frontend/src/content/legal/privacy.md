## Summary

MarketMosaic is an investment-research and education tool. This policy describes what the service stores about you, why, and for how long. It is written to match what the software actually does; where the software changes, this page changes with it.

## Who we are

MarketMosaic is operated by its owner (the "operator"). Contact details for privacy requests are published on this page once the operator has completed its legal review.

## What we collect and why

- **Account identity.** When you sign in, our sign-in provider (Clerk) issues a token that identifies you. We store the provider's user id and a one-way hash of your email address so that one email cannot start more than one trial. We do not store the email address itself; the app shows it from your sign-in session.
- **Plan and usage.** Your plan (Free Explorer, Pro trial, Pro), trial dates, subscription state and per-feature usage counters for the current calendar month, so that allowances can be enforced and shown to you.
- **Billing.** If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and the subscription's status. We never see or store card numbers.
- **Product events.** Page views and product actions (for example "opened the pricing page", "started a research run") tied to a random anonymous id stored in your browser, and to your account when you are signed in. These exist to understand how the product is used. No third-party analytics script runs on this site.
- **Request logs.** Standard server logs with request path, status code, timing and an IP address, kept for operations and abuse detection. On account creation we also store a salted hash of the IP address and browser user agent, not the raw values, so that repeated trial creation can be detected.
- **Your inputs.** Questions you ask in the app and the tickers you research are processed by AI model providers to produce an answer. Prompts are not written to our logs.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not run advertising or third-party tracking scripts on the marketing site.
- We do not build device fingerprints.
- We do not give personalised investment advice, and we do not store brokerage or bank credentials.

## Retention

- Product events are deleted after 90 days.
- Usage records and billing event records are kept for the life of the account as an audit trail of what was charged against your allowances.
- Rate-limit records expire within hours.
- When you delete your account with the sign-in provider, the operator removes the account record on request.

## Third parties

- **Clerk** — authentication. Runs only on the sign-in pages and inside the app, not on the marketing pages.
- **Stripe** — payments and the billing portal.
- **AI model providers** — process the prompts the app sends on your behalf to generate research.
- **Market-data providers and SEC EDGAR** — the sources of filings, fundamentals and prices.

Each of these has its own privacy policy governing the data it processes.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Requests go to the operator at the contact address above.

## Changes

Material changes will be dated on this page. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
