## Summary

Pro is a paid subscription billed monthly or yearly through Stripe. The trial is free and needs no card. These terms cover what is charged, when, and how to stop.

## Prices

The current prices are shown on the pricing page in US dollars. Taxes may be added at checkout where the operator is required to collect them.

## Trial

- Every new verified account starts a Pro trial automatically. No payment method is requested.
- When the trial ends, the account becomes Free Explorer. Nothing is charged.
- Subscribing during the trial: if at least 48 hours of trial remain, the remaining trial time is kept and the first charge happens when the trial would have ended. If less than 48 hours remain, billing starts at checkout. The checkout page states which applies before you confirm.

## Subscription

- Pro renews automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly) until cancelled.
- The billing period, renewal date and payment method are managed in the Stripe billing portal, reached from your account page.
- A completed checkout is confirmed by Stripe's notification to our servers, not by the return page. Your account shows the subscription once that confirmation arrives, normally within a minute; the account page offers a manual re-check.

## Cancellation

Cancel at any time from the billing portal. Cancellation stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid for, then the account returns to Free Explorer.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, the account keeps Pro for a short grace period while Stripe retries. If payment still fails, the account returns to Free Explorer until payment is updated.

## Refunds

The refund policy will be stated here once the operator's legal review is complete. Until then, refund requests are handled case by case by the operator.

## Allowance changes

Allowances shown on the pricing page may change with notice. A change never reduces what a paid period already started with.
