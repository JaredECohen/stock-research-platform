## What this site stores in your browser

MarketMosaic sets no advertising or tracking cookies and loads no third-party analytics scripts. Everything MarketMosaic's own code stores is listed below; the exact storage keys are printed under this text straight from the code that writes them. The one third-party script that can run on this site is our sign-in provider's, described in its own section.

## Marketing pages (signed out)

- **Anonymous id** — a random identifier in local storage, generated by your browser on first visit. Used to count product events (page views, sample views) without knowing who you are. It is never joined to a person unless you sign in.
- **Session id** — a random identifier in session storage, per browser tab, cleared when the tab closes. Used to group interface logs from one visit.

That is everything MarketMosaic's own code stores on the marketing pages. No fingerprinting, no cross-site identifiers.

## Sign-in provider (when accounts are enabled)

When accounts are enabled on this deployment, our sign-in provider (Clerk) loads on every page of this site, including the marketing pages, so the header can tell whether you are signed in. Its script is fetched from Clerk's servers, and it sets its own first-party cookies on this domain: one for every visitor, signed in or not, and one more once you sign in. Both are named in the table "Cookies set by the sign-in provider" under this text.

Their lifetimes are set by Clerk; they are not used for tracking. When accounts are disabled on a deployment, nothing from Clerk loads and neither cookie is set.

## The app (signed in)

Inside the app the following are added:

- **Interface preferences** — small values in local and session storage: your screener tab and rule set, whether you dismissed a status banner, and a marker that your account has been initialised this session.

## Third parties

- Clerk (sign-in) loads on every page of this site when accounts are enabled, including the marketing pages, and sets the cookies named in the table below.
- Stripe (payments) runs on Stripe's own checkout and billing-portal pages, not on this site.

## Managing storage

You can clear local and session storage from your browser settings at any time. Doing so resets the anonymous id and any saved preferences; it does not affect your account. Clerk's cookies are removed by signing out or by clearing this site's cookies.
