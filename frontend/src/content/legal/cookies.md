## What this site stores in your browser

MarketMosaic sets no advertising or tracking cookies and loads no third-party analytics scripts. Everything the marketing site stores is listed below; the exact storage keys are printed under this text straight from the code that writes them.

## Marketing pages (signed out)

- **Anonymous id** — a random identifier in local storage, generated by your browser on first visit. Used to count product events (page views, sample views) without knowing who you are. It is never joined to a person unless you sign in.
- **Session id** — a random identifier in session storage, per browser tab, cleared when the tab closes. Used to group interface logs from one visit.

That is the complete list for the marketing pages. No fingerprinting, no cross-site identifiers.

## The app (signed in)

Inside the app the following are added:

- **Sign-in session** — our sign-in provider (Clerk) sets its own first-party cookies on this domain to keep you signed in. Their names and lifetimes are set by Clerk; they are not used for tracking.
- **Interface preferences** — small values in local and session storage: your screener tab and rule set, whether you dismissed a status banner, and a marker that your account has been initialised this session.

## Third parties

- Clerk (sign-in) runs only on the sign-in pages and inside the app.
- Stripe (payments) runs on Stripe's own checkout and billing-portal pages, not on this site.

## Managing storage

You can clear local and session storage from your browser settings at any time. Doing so resets the anonymous id and any saved preferences; it does not affect your account.
