## Mandatory governing methodology for future sessions

For long-term compounder research, construct the thesis in this exact order:

**World change → industry consequences → durable company advantage → value capture → reinvestment runway → long-term per-share compounding → financial evidence/falsification → valuation.**

Treat each arrow as a claim to be tested. Record its causal mechanism, expected time horizon, necessary conditions, supporting evidence, plausible competing explanation and falsifier. Reason from external structural changes into future industry structure and company economics; a theme, screen result or attractive KPI is only a lead until the full chain is explicit. Evidence should test every link continuously, with the seventh stage serving as the formal financial audit.

Numbers are **evidence and checkpoints, not the thesis itself**. Use dated filings and operating data to establish base rates, test mechanisms, bound magnitude and timing, expose contradictions, measure price-implied expectations and define what would invalidate the thesis. Keep observed facts, causal inferences and forecast assumptions separate. Per-share compounding must be assessed after necessary reinvestment, dilution, debt, acquisitions, dividends and repurchases.

Do **not** use **current KPI → forecast KPI → EPS → target price** as the primary idea-generation or underwriting process. KPIs, forecasts, EPS, scenarios and target prices are valid downstream tools for testing, quantification, falsification and valuation only after the causal thesis is explicit. If a request begins with a ticker, KPI, screen, EPS estimate or target price, treat it as a lead and reconstruct the chain from the outside in before reaching a conclusion.

The shorter-term inflection track remains separate and valid. Apply the same causal discipline on its shorter horizon, including the evidence that the market is mispricing the change and the observation or event that would falsify it. Every future research handoff should state the strongest-supported link, the weakest or unresolved link, the decisive falsifier and the valuation assumptions.

## Required company evidence standard — clarified September 10, 2026

The user explicitly emphasizes 10-Ks, quarterly earnings reports and management discussion as the foundation for understanding updated performance, upcoming risks and current conditions. Apply this requirement to subsequent company investigations.

- **Latest annual filing:** read the business and segment discussion, MD&A, material risk factors, audited statements and relevant notes on revenue, working capital, debt, leases, acquisitions, equity compensation and other material claims. Compare prior-year disclosures where needed to identify what changed. An annual earnings release does not replace the annual filing.
- **Latest quarterly evidence:** pair the actual 10-Q with the quarterly earnings release, non-GAAP reconciliation and management discussion/call. For foreign issuers use the applicable annual and interim equivalents. A release, transcript, filing and presentation are different documents and must be recorded separately.
- **Performance explanation:** bridge revenue through volume, realized price, mix, currency and transactions; bridge margins through operating changes, temporary items and accounting; bridge earnings into cash after investment and financing. Record management's explanation as a claim and check it against statements, notes and industry evidence.
- **Forward risks and state:** identify changed risk language, demand/customer behavior, backlog quality, inventory, cost pressure, capital plans, debt maturities, covenants, dilution, regulatory milestones and guidance assumptions. Record the next observable test and plausible competing explanations.
- **Traceability:** save issuer/ticker, filing type, fiscal period, publication/access date, source URL, sections actually reviewed, findings and open questions. Label partial reading explicitly; only mark a full-document read when the whole document has actually been examined.
- **Coverage claims:** a company name or listing match is a research lead. Industry-framework coverage is separate from filing-backed company coverage. A selected-section review is separate from full reading and from a valuation-ready thesis. Do not treat links located but unread, failed retrievals or duplicated filings as additional company reviews.

Use this standard during the next depth investigations. The documented selected-section count does not certify that all 23 companies meet the expanded evidence standard.

## Cross-industry reasoning to retain

- Housing activity affects building products, furniture, mortgages, property services and storage with different lags; new construction and existing-home turnover can diverge.
- Computing demand must become funded, powered and delivered capacity before it creates cash for data centers, grid equipment, generators and semiconductor suppliers. Avoid counting one project multiple times.
- Producer commodity cash, service budgets, transport contracts and refining spreads are different stages with potentially opposite exposures.
- Crop balances, processor basis, ingredient price-cost, branded volumes and retail trade-down determine where food-chain value accrues.
- Distributor restocking differs from end-user demand. Freight mix and fuel pass-through differ from volume.
- Higher medical utilization may help providers and suppliers while hurting risk-bearing insurers; provider cash and tenant rent coverage are separate.
- Credit and refinancing stress require simultaneous operating-cash, collateral and capital tests.
- Audience engagement, advertising outcomes, retained fees and content/infrastructure spending determine platform economics.
- Productivity gains can accrue to workers, customers or suppliers; wage reductions alone are not evidence of durable efficiency.
- Insurance premiums, underwriting losses, broker commissions and insured-property expenses are connected but not equivalent.
- Resource demand narratives must be tested against substitution, recycling, new capacity and extraction economics.
- Backlog, contracts, eligible lives, regulatory authorization and booked megawatts all precede cash; financing and dilution cannot be omitted.
