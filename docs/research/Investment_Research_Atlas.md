## 2. How to use the atlas

Start with the economic mechanism, not a ticker. Each node traces who pays whom, how profits arise, what constrains supply, which observable test matters and which evidence invalidates the idea. Then examine representative companies as possible beneficiaries, competitors, customers or industry anchors.

Use the indicator definition before interpreting a number. The ledger distinguishes period-end from publication date, percent from percentage points, and reported values from management estimates. Blank means not obtained, not zero. The workbook computes age, changes and limited normalization bridges from linked inputs.

Read the historical counterexample before upgrading conviction. A technology can succeed while suppliers earn poor returns; orders can accelerate before working-capital needs become visible; inventory can turn before prices stabilize; and a reported profit rebound can reflect refunds rather than improving demand.

Finally, use a screen as a question queue. No current market capitalization, share price, forward multiple, licensed consensus estimate or reverse-valuation result has been inserted without verification. This snapshot does not establish that a candidate is cheap, small-cap eligible or actionable.

## 4. Research scoring, gates and uncertainty

The score is a deliberately transparent allocation of research effort. Four analyst judgments range from 1 to 5: strategy fit, potential decision impact, specificity of existing evidence and testability of the next question.

**Compounder score = 20 × (35% fit + 15% decision impact + 25% evidence + 25% testability).**

**Inflection score = 20 × (20% fit + 30% decision impact + 25% evidence + 25% testability).**

These weights are editable and unvalidated. A one-point difference is not statistically meaningful. Ranks are within strategy, not across the entire market; ties remain ties. Evidence holds receive no rank. A high score can mean that resolving a serious objection is unusually valuable, not that the expected investment outcome is unusually favorable.

Before a stock becomes actionable, its security identity, share count and claims, capital structure, normalized operating model, price-implied outcomes, liquidity, independent bear case and portfolio suitability must be verified. A full consensus data source has not been connected; management guidance is not a substitute for consensus, and a reverse valuation is a range of assumptions rather than a single observable market belief.

A probability-weighted valuation should be derived from internally coherent scenarios. Growth, margins, capital expenditure, working capital, debt and diluted shares must fit together. Do not average scenario IRRs and call that the portfolio's expected annual return; distinguish expected terminal wealth, annualized scenario returns and downside distributions.

## 5. Indicator governance

A useful indicator has an economic hypothesis, a specific measurement, a publication lag, a proposed test, an earnings transmission mechanism and an identified false positive. The 65 definitions have these fields; their numerical thresholds are proposed research controls, not empirically optimized trading rules.

Monthly and quarterly review-age thresholds in Settings are editable operational defaults. An observation being within its review window does not establish predictive validity. Every actual use must check whether a newer release exists, whether the reported period matches the model, and whether revised data were available at the historical decision date.

The signal board intentionally preserves conflicting observations: housing starts and permits can diverge; bookings and shipments can point in different directions; earned and written premiums can have opposite growth signs. A composite green/red signal should not conceal those differences.

## 6. Historical validation protocol

The 24 episodes establish documented mechanisms and failure modes. They do not establish excess returns, an optimal entry date or that an insight was available before the stock moved.

For a real test, register the hypothesis, eligible universe, observation source, time lag, threshold, entry convention, exit rule, transaction cost and benchmark before evaluating results. Use original data vintages and announcement timestamps where possible. Include delisted companies, abandoned projects and rejected ideas; preserve split, dividend, currency and corporate-action histories. Test across non-overlapping cycles and use an out-of-sample period. Report uncertainty, drawdowns, base rates, turnover and the entire distribution—not just a memorable winner.

Clinical and regulatory histories require especially careful labeling: a later commercial discontinuation does not retrospectively prove that a trial was fraudulent or that every earlier clinical conclusion was false. Funding, reimbursement, competition and strategic choices can change commercial outcomes independently.

## 7. Cross-sector risk and the economic graph

GICS helps organize coverage but does not diversify a thesis by itself. Cooling, switchgear, optics, electrical contracting and data-center real estate can all depend on the same funded capital program. Likewise, homebuilders, flooring, mortgage finance and apartment supply can share rate and household-formation exposures.

The 27 links identify such pathways, the intermediate constraint and the evidence that would break the chain. They are proposed causal maps, not measured correlations or position-size recommendations. Concentration should be measured by common customers, financing, inputs and demand drivers as well as ticker and sector.

## 8. Corrections built into the process

Demand quality is multidimensional: legal enforceability, cancellation rights, customer funding, delivery conditions, deposits, pricing resets and remedies. Revenue, RPO, orders, reservations, letters of intent and opportunity pipelines can overlap. Never add them together or treat a simple hierarchy as a universal probability scale.

For a regulated utility, the equity-return component is approximately rate base × allowed equity fraction × allowed ROE, not rate base × ROE alone. Recovery, regulatory lag, debt costs and disallowances still matter.

Percentage earnings elasticity becomes unstable near zero or negative earnings. Use dollar sensitivity to utilization, volume, price and gross margin with a full cost/cash bridge in those cases.

One good quarterly report does not prove a moat. Renewal and repeat buying support customer value but do not replace unit economics; recurring revenue without disconnect/retention evidence receives partial credit. Asset-light structures can still require software investment, sales spending, acquisitions and dilution.

## 9. File organization and maintenance

The workbook contains 18 sheets with formula-linked scores, ranks, freshness, gates and normalization checks. The standalone HTML explorer searches and filters the same datasets without an external service. The package includes CSV and JSON for every layer, 11 sector dossiers, the candidate and historical libraries, the source registry and validation results.

This snapshot supersedes unsupported earlier price/multiple rankings. It does not silently refresh them. Changes should be append-only in the observation and decision ledgers: preserve what was known, when it was known, why an assumption changed and whether the price changed too.

The next highest-value work is security/valuation normalization across the serious candidates, followed by the decisive company-specific questions. Breadth is already sufficient to avoid an AI-only search; the next unit of effort should improve the quality of a decision rather than increase the name count.
